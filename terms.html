ShortsLab is a desktop application operated by the owner of the ShortsLab YouTube channel. It generates original short videos and can schedule and upload them to the channel selected during Google authorization.

Use ShortsLab only with a YouTube channel you are authorized to manage. Your use of YouTube API Services through ShortsLab is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. The channel owner controls the account, publication settings, and content on the channel.

You can stop the Windows scheduled task and revoke access in Google Account connections. To change an upload that was already scheduled, use YouTube Studio.
